# Day 1 - Phishing Basics

## What is Phishing
Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers impersonate trusted organizations or individuals to steal sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, banking details, or login credentials.

## Common Goals of Phishing
- Stealing passwords
- Financial fraud
- Identity theft
- Malware installation

## Types of Phishing

### Email Phishing
Fake emails pretending to be from banks, companies, or trusted services.

### Smishing
Phishing attacks through SMS messages.

### Vishing
Voice-call phishing where attackers pretend to be customer support or officials.

### Spear Phishing
Targeted phishing attacks personalized for specific individuals.

### Whaling
Phishing attacks targeting high-profile executives or senior management.

## Social Engineering
Phishing attacks use psychological manipulation such as:
- Fear
- Urgency
- Excitement
- Curiosity

to trick victims into acting quickly.

## Red Flags of Phishing Emails
- Suspicious sender address
- Urgent or threatening language
- Generic greetings
- Suspicious links
- Grammar and spelling mistakes
- Requests for passwords or personal information

## Safe Browsing Practices
- Always check URLs carefully
- Use HTTPS websites
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Avoid clicking suspicious links
- Verify requests through official sources

## Real-World Example
The 2020 Twitter breach happened due to phishing and social engineering attacks targeting employees.

## Key Learning
Phishing attacks target human behavior more than technology. Awareness and verification are the best defenses.
